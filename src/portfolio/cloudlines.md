As a freelancer for Manada I developed the look of Cloudlines: I created the shaders,
optimized them, and set up a workflow so the team could make new assets and iterate quickly
whenever the art direction changed.

Most meshes were already final, so the challenge was to get an interesting look out of as few
resources as possible.
